## Task

Develop distinct product storytelling angles grounded in verified product information. Separate product features, plausible benefits, and unverified hypotheses. Do not invent customer testimonials, usage statistics, guarantees, or competitive superiority.

## Output

1. One-paragraph positioning draft.
2. Three content angles, each with:
   - Target audience need
   - Main message
   - Verified supporting facts
   - Missing evidence
   - Suggested format
3. For each approved angle:
   - Hook
   - Short script or post copy
   - Caption
   - CTA
4. Objection-handling drafts based only on supplied facts.
5. Claims register:
   - Exact claim
   - Evidence
   - Risk / uncertainty
   - Keep, qualify, or remove recommendation
6. Assumptions and questions for the customer.
7. Self-review against the Embrilo QC rubric.

## Constraints

- No invented claims, testimonials, reviews, certifications, statistics, or before/after results.
- Avoid unsubstantiated words such as “best,” “number one,” “guaranteed,” or “clinically proven.”
- No misleading scarcity or urgency.
- Do not reuse competitor wording.
- Mark concept examples clearly.
- Human review is mandatory before delivery.
